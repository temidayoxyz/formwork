The studio

Forty-eight people, one drawing floor

Formwork was founded in 2009 by Mette Kjær and Jonas Brandt in a former shipyard weld shop on Refshaleøen. The studio works on civic and cultural buildings — projects where architecture is a public obligation before it is a private art.

How we work

Every project begins with use, not form: who enters, how long they stay, what they carry, what they leave with. The answers become diagrams before they become buildings. Materials are chosen for their second life — cast timber, reclaimed brick, low-clinker concrete.

The studio maintains an open model workshop and a materials library of 3,000 samples. Both are used weekly by the schools of architecture in Copenhagen and Aarhus.
